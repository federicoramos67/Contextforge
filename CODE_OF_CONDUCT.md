# Code of Conduct

**English** · [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

## Our goal

ContextForge aims to be a respectful, collaborative and constructive place to
work.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus on ideas rather than people.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Help improve documentation and shared understanding.
- Be patient with people writing in their second language. This project is
  bilingual, and imperfect English or Spanish is never a reason to dismiss
  someone's contribution.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Spam or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Sharing other people's private credentials or data.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and documentation
contributions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate
these guidelines, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer directly rather than escalating in
public.
